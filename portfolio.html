Launched Product • Career & AI Tooling

TheResumeChef

https://theresumechef.com

Built after experiencing firsthand the frustration of updating resumes in Microsoft Word and Canva—and watching job seekers get hit with sneaky $29.99/month subscription paywalls just to download their own PDF. TheResumeChef treats career history like structured ingredients, letting users switch between designer templates and export crisp, ATS-friendly vector PDFs 100% free.

Key Capabilities

  • 100% Free Forever Studio: Instant live preview, 10 chef-curated layouts, custom typography, and 1-click vector PDF export with zero watermarks.
  • Cloud Pantry Sync: Save portable JSON resume bundles locally or sync directly to Dropbox & Google Drive without mandatory account sign-up.
  • Resume Extraction Kitchen: Drag-and-drop PDF, DOCX, and TXT parser extracting employment history, education, and skills into structured JSON.
  • Tech Job Board & MCP Server: Candidate matching engine plus a JSON-RPC 2.0 Model Context Protocol (MCP) API enabling AI agents (Claude, Cursor, Antigravity) to post and manage listings.

Architecture & Stack

  • Backend: Rust (Axum) serving high-performance REST & MCP APIs plus embedded static assets.
  • Frontend: React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS.
  • Infrastructure: Containerized with Podman/Docker, Traefik v3 reverse proxy with native HTTP/3 (QUIC), Cloudflare Edge, and Authelia SSO admin dashboard.
  • Integrations: Stripe subscriptions for AI agent access, Google AdSense (non-intrusive banner monetization), Dropbox & Google Drive OAuth.

Launched Product • Security, QR & Analytics

getdaisylink

https://getdaisylink.com & https://daisyl.ink

A tamper-resistant, blockchain-anchored URL shortener, customizable QR code studio, and opt-in privacy-first link analytics platform. Designed as a trustworthy, transparent alternative to pricey link-management SaaS tools.

Key Capabilities

  • Tamper-Resistant Short Links: Computes a canonical SHA-256 hash of (slug, destinationUrl, createdAt) and signs an EIP-191 block attestation against Base Mainnet; every redirect verifies integrity in real time.
  • Customizable QR Code Studio: Generates HD SVG, PNG, and JPEG QR codes with custom module patterns, corner eyes, colors, frames, and watermarks.
  • Opt-In Privacy-First Analytics: Free anonymous links record zero click telemetry; authenticated users (Google & Dropbox SSO, zero local passwords) can opt in for click, QR scan, geo, referrer, and UTM insights.
  • Abuse & Spam Hardening: SSRF URL validation, IPv6 /64 quota bucketing, Cloudflare Turnstile bot protection, and Resend email receipts.

Architecture & Stack

  • Runtime & Data: TypeScript, Node.js, SQLite with 6-hourly atomic S3 backups and 90-day lifecycle rules.
  • Blockchain: Base Mainnet (Chain ID 8453) gasless EIP-191 attestation with 15s block caching.
  • Observability & Ops: Internal /metrics scraped by VictoriaMetrics, Grafana unified alerting, external synthetic watchdog cron, and blue-green container cutover via Traefik.
  • Testing: Comprehensive automated unit & integration test suite (203 tests across 13 files).
